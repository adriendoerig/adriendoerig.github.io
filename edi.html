Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion

Science, like all human endeavours, thrives from diverse backgrounds and experiences, coming together in a respectful, curious and stimulating manner. Labs should be places where inclusivity, honesty, science and critical thinking rule over hierarchy. Our group is committed to these values, and to actively fighting discrimination.

We recognise that discrimination can be based on a broad range of factors: race, gender, sexual orientation, religion, age, disability, and many more. We are committed to actively work against such biases to make our lab, and science at large, a more equitable, diverse, and inclusive space. This involves all aspects of lab-life, including day-to-day lab practices, recruitment, training, and development opportunities. Discrimination has no place in science and in our group.

Feel free to contact individual lab members to learn more about our group and how it operates.
